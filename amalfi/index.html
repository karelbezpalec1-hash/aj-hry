Amalfi Coast · summer 2024 · escape game

Save the Last Lemon Tree

Francesco Bonacore grows lemons on the Amalfi Coast. You met him in the DW video. This year, mal secco is killing his trees.

The cure is in his old shed, behind seven locks. Open them all and his last tree lives.

Work in pairs. When you finish, your time goes into the Hall of Fame. A hint costs +30 seconds.

Use a team nickname, not your real full name. The whole class will see it in the Hall of Fame.

Lock 1 of 7 · word lock

Five words, five letters

Write the words from the worksheet. Their first letters open the lock.

Lock 2 of 7 · number lock

Lemon crates

Some sentences are true. Add up the numbers of the true ones. That's the code.

  • 12Amalfi lemons are small and round.
  • 25Mal secco is caused by a fungus.
  • 8Harvesting on the terraces is still done mostly by hand.
  • 40A sick tree can always be saved if the farmer acts fast.
  • 100Amalfi lemons are used to make limoncello.
  • 50A young lemon tree gives a big harvest in its first year.

Exactly three sentences are true.
Lock 3 of 7 · anagram lock

Mixed-up lemon words

The wind has mixed up the letters. Write the real words.

First letters: O… F… H… T…
Lock 4 of 7 · partner lock

Word partners

These words go together in the text. Tap a word on the left, then its partner on the right.

Three of them: fungal disease, cultural heritage, early detection.
Lock 5 of 7 · basket lock

Francesco's story

Fill the gaps one by one. Tap the right word in the lemon basket. One word is extra.

Fragrant = it smells nice. Livelihood = the way you earn money to live. The extra word is landslides.
Lock 6 of 7 · voices lock

Who said it?

Five people talk about the lemons. Who said each sentence?

The scientist talks about the infection. The mayor talks about the town.

Problem or solution?

Sort all eight cards. A wrong choice shakes the card.
